About Grason

Grason Crabtree is a non-union voice actor based in Oregon, who's been working in the industry since 2016. He’s had the chance to voice dozens of different characters in video games and animation for clients all over the world. Always looking for new creative challenges. Whether it's experimenting with different voices or diving into new genres, excited about every opportunity to grow and have fun in the process.
